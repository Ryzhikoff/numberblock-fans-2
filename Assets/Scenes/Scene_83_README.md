# Scene 83 — NumberBlocks 10 to 1000: Collision Chain

Aspect-responsive video with a perspective camera and a block-built Minecraft-style arena.
The camera automatically follows the Game View or Recorder resolution without stretching;
horizontal 16:9 is the primary framing.

## Sequence

- Tens: `10 + 10 = 20`, then `10 + 20`, continuing to `10 + 90 = 100`.
- Hundreds: `100 + 100 = 200`, then `100 + 200`, continuing to `100 + 900 = 1000`.
- Every addition uses two impacts: an initial hit and rebound, followed by a harder
  second charge that scatters both characters into exact original Ones.
- All 18 results reassemble from the exact unit count, from 20 through 1000.
- The full runtime is approximately 162 seconds: 18 additions, 36 physical impacts,
  and 18 scatter/reassembly beats.

## Custom expressions

Scene-local transparent overlays are used for determined/surprised Number 10,
surprised 50 and 100, shocked 500, and victorious 1000. Original block prefabs and
materials are not modified.

## Editor tools

- `Tools/Scene_83/Quick Setup 10 To 1000 Collision Journey`
- `Tools/Scene_83/Validate 10 To 1000 Short`

Use a 16:9 Game View for the primary horizontal video. Other aspect ratios remain unstretched.
